Support that gets to the point.

Support email: millerlabchannel@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, include the device model, Android version, app mode, exact steps and any screenshot or log that does not contain private information.

How do I start a workout?

Open RepPulse, choose Tabata, Countdown or Stopwatch from the home screen, configure the session if needed, then use the primary start control.

How do I configure a Tabata workout?

Choose Tabata, set preparation, work, rest and round values, review the next phase, then start. The timer supports portrait and landscape layouts while the session is active.

How do I use Countdown?

Open Countdown, select a saved session or create one, configure its duration and start it. Saved Countdown sessions remain local to the device.

How do I use Multi Countdown?

Open the Countdown library, create or select the saved Countdown sessions you want to use, and follow the session controls. Multi Countdown data is stored locally; it does not sync to a RepPulse account.

How do I use the Stopwatch?

Choose Stopwatch from the home screen. Start, pause/resume, record laps and reset using the visible controls.

How do I configure audio signals?

Open Settings and adjust training sounds, volume, vibration and countdown feedback. These preferences are stored locally on the device.

How do I change privacy preferences?

Open Settings → Privacy & Data. When Google UMP requires privacy options, use the official privacy-options form. The app does not force consent or treat its own local setting as a replacement for Google’s consent state.

How do I report a problem?

Email millerlabchannel@gmail.com with the device model, Android version, app mode and reproduction steps.

How do I request information or exercise privacy rights?

Contact millerlabchannel@gmail.com. Explain the request and provide only the information needed to identify it. The privacy policy describes the available rights and local deletion controls.

Come si configura un allenamento Tabata?

Scegli Tabata, imposta preparazione, lavoro, pausa e round, controlla la fase successiva e avvia il timer.

Come funzionano Countdown, Multi Countdown e Cronometro?

Countdown gestisce sessioni salvate, Multi Countdown permette di usare più sessioni locali, mentre Cronometro offre avvio, pausa/ripresa, lap e reset.

Come modifico audio, privacy o diritti?

Le preferenze audio sono in Settings. Le opzioni privacy sono in Settings → Privacy & Data quando richieste da UMP. Per richieste privacy scrivi all’indirizzo email indicato sopra.
