Privacy Policy

Publication status: pending final review. The controller identity and public contact are now configured. Publication still requires confirmation of the final Google AdMob/UMP configuration, retention wording, legal review and the final public deployment.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller is Miller Alberto Della Morte, an individual developer operating under the Miller Lab brand, Italy. No physical address is published on this page. For privacy requests and the exercise of data-protection rights, contact millerlabchannel@gmail.com.

2. The RepPulse application

RepPulse is an Android training timer with Tabata/interval workouts, saved Countdown sessions, Multi Countdown, Stopwatch mode and configurable audio, volume and vibration feedback. The app does not require an account for its core timer features. The app is currently marked as coming soon to Google Play; the Google Play URL will be added when available.

3. Data processed

The app stores workout names and configurations, Countdown configurations, language and feedback preferences, and privacy/advertising-mode settings locally on the device. In DEBUG builds, developer advertising diagnostics and test controls may also be stored locally. The RepPulse code does not send these workout settings to a RepPulse backend.

When Google advertising services are enabled, Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform may process technical, device and advertising-related information according to the active AdMob configuration, the consent choices shown to the user and Google’s documentation.

4. Local storage and backup

Local preferences are implemented with Android DataStore Preferences and SharedPreferences. Android backup is enabled in the application manifest; the exact backup scope and treatment of backed-up preferences must be confirmed against the final release backup rules and Google Play Data Safety declaration. Users should use the Android system controls or uninstall the app to remove app-local data, subject to the device’s backup behavior.

5. Purposes and legal bases

Local processing supports the timer, workout configuration, feedback preferences, language and privacy settings requested by the user. The controller’s legal basis for these core operations and for any advertising-related processing must be confirmed in the final legal review. Advertising requests are subject to the Google UMP consent state and are not represented as active in the OFF or SIMULATED modes.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

RepPulse contains an integration with Google Mobile Ads for banners and workout-completion interstitials. OFF disables advertising requests. SIMULATED uses an offline local surface and does not request Google ads. ADMOB TEST uses official Google demo ad units and is intended for development/testing; real identifiers in private configuration do not by themselves enable production monetization. Release monetization remains disabled until deliberately configured and reviewed.

Official references: Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure guidance.

7. Consent with Google UMP

Where required, RepPulse requests consent information through Google User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. The app uses canRequestAds() as the gate and exposes the official privacy-options form when required. UMP errors or unavailable network services can prevent advertising requests; the app does not force consent. See the official UMP Android documentation.

8. Recipients and international transfers

For the core timer, workout settings remain in the app’s local storage. If advertising is enabled, Google and its service providers may receive information necessary for the advertising and consent services. The applicable recipients, transfer mechanisms and safeguards must be checked against the final Google configuration and Google’s current terms and privacy documentation before publication.

9. Retention

RepPulse does not define a separate server-side retention period for workout settings because it has no RepPulse backend. Local data remains subject to Android app storage and user/device controls. Google service retention is governed by the applicable Google service configuration and policies. The controller must confirm the final wording and any applicable periods before publication.

10. Rights and deletion

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, or raise a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. Contact millerlabchannel@gmail.com. App-local data can be removed using Android’s app-data controls or by uninstalling the app; advertising/privacy choices can be managed through the available in-app privacy options where UMP requires them.

11. Children

RepPulse is not designed as a service directed to children. The controller must confirm the intended audience and any age-related Google Play declarations before publication.

12. Website and email support

This site is intended to be a static GitHub Pages website. It does not intentionally add analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical request data as part of hosting, and email providers process messages sent to the support address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and your email provider’s terms.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, SDK configuration or legal information changes. The effective date appears at the top of this page.

14. Contact

Privacy contact: millerlabchannel@gmail.com.
